End of Week

A shorter cadence between Daily and Monthly — a weekly gut-check on what moved and what didn't.

Reflection Practice Weekly cadence Try It

Four prompts, once a week:

  • Wins — what got done this week that you're genuinely happy with?
  • Carryover — what's still open, and should it move to next week or get dropped?
  • Patterns — anything that kept coming up: a recurring blocker, a recurring win?

Next Week's Focus — what's the one thing that has to happen next week? Set it during the Weekly Planning Ritual.

Try It

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude, or whatever AI tool you use:

Help me run an End of Week check-in using four prompts: Wins, Carryover, Patterns, and Next Week's Focus. Ask me one at a time and push me to name the one thing that has to happen next week.
