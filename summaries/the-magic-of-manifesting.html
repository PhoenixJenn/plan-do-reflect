The Magic of Manifesting

15 Advanced Techniques to Attract Your Best Life, Even If You Think It's Impossible Now

by Ryuu Shinohara

2020 · 3h 36m audiobook · ★ 4.8 Audible

The big idea

The book presents manifestation as aligning your personal vibrational frequency with what you want to attract, through present-moment focus, detachment from outcomes, and visualization, blending Law of Attraction ideas with references to quantum physics and heart and brain electromagnetic fields.

Vibrational matching
Aggregator summaries describe the core technique as consciously choosing thoughts and feelings that match the frequency of what you want, on the premise that like attracts like, requiring detachment from anxious attachment to a specific outcome.

No independent author bio, credentials, or media coverage could be verified for Ryuu Shinohara, who has published dozens of similarly titled manifesting books; treat this summary as what aggregator sites describe, not as independently verified reporting of the source text.

Key ideas

  1. Present-moment focus

    Summaries describe the book urging readers to treat now as the only real point of leverage, deprioritizing past regret or future anxiety.

  2. Detachment from outcomes

    Release attachment or worry about a specific result, on the theory that anxious resistance blocks what you're trying to attract.

  3. Visualization

    Use multisensory mental imagery to rehearse a desired outcome as if it's already happened.

  4. Abundance mindset

    Practice gratitude and generosity to shift away from scarcity-based thinking.
