SMART Goals

Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound. Individual by nature — less a framework than a filter you run your own goal through before committing time to it, and a vague intention ("get better at this") turns into something you can actually plan around and know when you've hit.

OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)

Native habitat is org or team alignment — a small set of shared objectives with measurable key results everyone can see progress against. Worth borrowing at the individual level too: pick 1–3 personal objectives and 3–5 measurable key results per objective, and a goal stops being a wish the moment it has a number attached. If SMART Goals is how you check one goal on your own, OKRs is how a goal cascades — from the org's, to the team's, to yours.
