Organization

The same cycle, run at company scale — where Reflect becomes Inspect, a shared review instead of a private habit.

The planning-and-review cadence a whole organization runs on — its own Plan, Do, and Reflect, below. Strategic Planning and the Do group's execution-cadence pages moved here from Core Skills and Team respectively — both are already company-scoped, not project-scoped, so they belong with the org cadence rather than sitting one level down. The people-leadership side of running an org — Leading, Managing, People Management — moved out to its own Leadership section instead, since it's about how you operate, the same altitude as Self, not about the org's cadence.

Not One-and-Done

Planning itself is incremental, not a single annual event. OP1 shapes the Annual Operating Plan around H1; OP2 refines it at H2/EOY once the year's actuals are in. What Reflect surfaces this quarter feeds next year's OP1 — the wheel keeps turning, it doesn't reset.
