Leadership

Self, zoomed out: how you operate once you're responsible for other people's work, not just your own.

Self and Leadership are both about how you operate — Self on your own output, Leadership on everyone else's. Team and Organization are the other pairing: how the work itself gets organized, at project scale and company scale. Leading is the developmental arc underneath it all — knowing yourself, thinking clearly, working through others, growing other leaders, and leading through hard terrain.

Plan

Decide how the team is shaped and what you want from the people on it, before the cycle starts.

Org Design

Shape the team before you run it — roles, reporting lines, and who owns what. Succession Planning lives here too: who's ready to grow into what, before you need the answer.

Goal Setting

Team-scale goal setting — what each person on your team is aiming for this cycle, and how it ladders up.

Development Plans

Each person's growth trajectory for the period — a different conversation than Goal Setting's performance and output targets.

Do

Run the team and the people on it, day to day.

Managing

The operational side of having a team: who's on it, what matters most, how work gets built, how it runs day to day. 1:1s live here.

People Management

Hiring, onboarding, and comp philosophy — the operational side of having people, not just a team.

Reflect

Stop and look back at how each person is actually doing.

Performance Reviews

The periodic, formal look back — distinct from the ongoing 1:1 cadence under Managing.
