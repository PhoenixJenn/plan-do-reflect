The framework behind this site

Plan · Do · Reflect

Why it matters

Experience alone doesn't make you better. You can spend ten years doing the same one year of work if you never stop to look at it. Researchers who study workplace learning keep finding the same thing: people who pause to think about what they just did outperform people who just keep doing. Reflection is what turns effort into growth. It's the difference between racking up hours and racking up wisdom.

What it is

Plan-Do-Reflect works as a flywheel, not a checklist: three moves that feed each other in a loop.

  • Plan: decide what you're aiming for, and why, before you start.
  • Do: do the actual work, on purpose, not on autopilot.
  • Reflect: stop and ask what happened, what worked, and what to change.

Each turn makes the next one easier. Small, consistent rotations build more momentum than one heroic push ever will.

How this site supports it

Most resources only help with the Do part. This one is built around all three stages. Browse a growing catalog of frameworks and tools organized by where you are in the cycle and what you're working on, from leading and managing to strategic planning. Get a Tip of the Day worth trying today. Build a Learning Plan that strings the right pieces together for whatever you're actually trying to get better at.

When to use it

The cycle works at any scale: daily, in a two-minute end-of-day check-in; weekly, in a proper review of what moved and what didn't; and at the big turning points, after a launch, a hard conversation, a promotion, or a setback. You don't need a special occasion to start. The best time to run your first cycle is whatever you're doing right now.
