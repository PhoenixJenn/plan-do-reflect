Core Skills

Not a stage in the cycle, and not scoped to one altitude — skills that support all of it, whether you're planning, doing, or reflecting, alone or with a whole organization around you.

Everything under Self is about running yourself; Leadership now holds Leading and Managing, and Organization holds Strategic Planning — the skills that scale with headcount. What's left here doesn't scale with altitude at all — Communication and AI Fundamentals matter exactly as much whether you're working alone or running a whole company, which is why they're grouped on their own below rather than under any one level.
