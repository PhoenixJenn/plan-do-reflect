Core Skills

Not a stage in the cycle — skills that support all of it, whether you're planning, doing, or reflecting.

Root category 5 categories

Everything under The Framework is about running yourself. This is about what you do once other people are involved — getting a message across clearly, working with AI well enough that it's actually useful, and leading, managing, or planning at a scale bigger than your own to-do list.

Foundational Skills

The two skills everything else on this list assumes you already have some of.

Communication

Written communication, storytelling, public speaking, and networking — ordered by how a message actually moves from you to someone else.

AI Fundamentals

A learning path, not a single skill: understand it, learn to use it, apply it to your work, then make it habitual.

Leadership & Management

The three domains that kick in once your job is no longer just about your own output.

Leading

A developmental arc — know yourself, think clearly, work through others, grow other leaders, lead through hard terrain.

Managing

The operational side of having a team: who's on it, what matters most, how work gets built, how it runs day to day.

Strategic Planning

The planning cycle zoomed out further still: scan the landscape, formulate, cascade into plans, measure, adjust.
