Self

The part of the cycle nobody assigns to you — how you run yourself before you try to run anything else.

Root category 3 stages

Every other area on this site — including Leading, Managing, and Strategic Planning under Organization — assumes you've already got a handle on running yourself. This is that handle. It's also the only root that's genuinely cyclical: Reflection feeds Planning, Planning feeds Focus and Time Management, and when the whole system wobbles, Motivation is what keeps you doing the work anyway.

Tools

Knowing the cycle isn't the same as running it. Plan-Do-Reflect only sticks if you've got somewhere to actually put it — a place to write down what you're aiming for, log what you actually did, and look back before you plan again. Without a mechanism like that, "reflect" stays a vague intention instead of something that happens on a schedule.

One concrete way to build that mechanism: Bullet Journaling — its Monthly Log, Daily Log, and Migration map onto Plan, Do, and Reflect almost exactly. Digital, paper, or something else works too — the format matters less than having a habit of writing it down at all.
